Option 1 — Essential Bar

  1. 1
    Colour button — 48px circle showing focus colour. Tap to open colour picker sheet.
  2. 2
    Find next — jumps crosshair to next remaining stitch of focus colour.
  3. 3
    Zoom Fit — one-tap fit-to-canvas. Replaces separate +/−/fit buttons on phone.
  4. 4
    Mode toggle — switches between Mark mode and Navigate mode.
  5. 5
    Pause — pauses / resumes the session timer.
  6. 6
    Progress chip — shows % and mini-bar. Tap for full breakdown.
  7. 7
    Undo — always-visible undo. Reverses last mark or drag-mark group.
  8. 8
    More button — opens "All Controls" drawer: session, view, highlight, layers, tools, notes.
  9. 9
    Pattern canvas — tap to mark/unmark. Long-press 300ms → drag mode activates.
  10. 10
    Collapse tab — tap the notch above the bar to retract it to a 12px handle; swipe up to restore.
Key changes from current UI:
Floating dock retired. Toolbar pill simplified. ··· overflow replaced by single "More" drawer. 30+ overflow items reorganised into 4 sections. Drag-mark now requires 300ms hold (no accidental drags while panning).
