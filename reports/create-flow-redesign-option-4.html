Design decisions
1
Pattern appears immediately on upload — pipeline runs at defaults within ~500 ms. No Generate button to click. Users see a result before they've touched any settings.
2
Dimensions first in sidebar — most impactful setting placed at the top where it will be changed most often.
3
"Draft" mode tag — the sidebar header always shows the current state. While settings are editable, users know they are in the pre-commit phase.
4
"Updating preview..." indicator — appears within 100 ms of any setting change, disappears when the update completes. Tells the user the canvas reflects the new setting.
5
"Lock & Edit" is the only commit action — after clicking, the settings sidebar is hidden (or locked), the toolstrip becomes active, and edit tools become available. No ambiguity.
6
Edit tools greyed out in draft mode — the toolstrip is visually disabled (0.35 opacity) until the pattern is locked. Users understand they cannot paint on a draft.
