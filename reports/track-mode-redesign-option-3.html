Option 3 — Split Palette

  1. 1
    Active colour tile — pinned at top of palette. Shows progress arc, DMC name, stitch count, and "Find next" button. Always visible on tablet/desktop.
  2. 2
    Colour tile list — large 48px tiles (swatch + name + progress bar + count). Tap to switch focus colour. Sorted by remaining stitches by default.
  3. 3
    Pattern canvas — tap to toggle (same as current). Long-press 300ms → drag mode. Canvas gets maximum space since all colour info is in the palette panel.
  4. 4
    Floating mode strip — 5 buttons (Mark, Nav, Fit, Undo, More), each 52×52px. Right-edge anchored. Does not obscure stitches — placed in the margin.
  5. 5
    Colour chip trigger (phone) — always visible at top of canvas. Tap to open palette panel slide-in. Shows active colour + DMC ID.
  6. 6
    Undo + More (canvas topbar, phone) — always reachable without opening palette panel.
Key changes from current UI:
Right panel legend replaced by large interactive palette tiles. No toolbar pill. No floating dock. No sidebar tabs. Colour switching is the primary action — the palette panel IS the sidebar. Mode strip replaces toolbar for active controls.
