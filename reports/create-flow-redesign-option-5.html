Design decisions
1
Tab bar is always visible — it is part of the permanent page chrome, not a modal or overlay. Users always know which phase they are in and can switch explicitly.
2
Live preview in Convert tab — the comparison pane (original vs. pattern) updates on every setting change. No need to click Generate to see a rough result.
3
All essential controls in Convert sidebar — dimensions, palette, stash, blends, background, smoothing, cleanup. No accordion needed; the tab separation gives enough space.
4
"Generate Pattern" is the only CTA that crosses modes — after clicking, the Edit tab activates automatically and the canvas shows the result. The tab switch is announced (brief toast: "Pattern generated — now editing").
5
Switching back to Convert shows a warning only if edits exist — the modal explains that settings are preserved but new generation will replace the current pattern.
6
Edit tab has no conversion controls — toolstrip and palette only. Zero bleed from Convert. The "Back to Convert" link is small and in the sidebar footer, never accidentally clicked.
7
Palette sidebar in Edit — stash dots show which threads you own. Colour replace and swap tools are accessible from the palette context menu.
