About this prototype

A prototype of a work area mode for the Stitch Tracker: pick one section of a large pattern and the chart, colour list, counts and bulk actions only cover that section. The pattern is generated and nothing is saved.

Try this

  1. Switch to 600 × 800, zoom right out, and look at the draw readout bottom-left.
  2. Pick a work area. The first unfinished block is already part-stitched.
  3. Tap stitches, or highlight a colour in the list and use Mark all done. It only covers the area.
  4. Finish the area to get the next-area prompt, or step with the arrow buttons. Finished areas are skipped.
  5. Change the margin to see how much surrounding context helps when lining up edges.

Choices made here

  • Clip, not dim. Cells beyond the margin are not drawn at all, and margin cells are view-only.
  • Scoped colour list. Only colours in the area are listed, with area counts.
  • Both progress figures. Area progress leads; whole-pattern progress stays visible.
  • Stepping skips finished areas and moves in reading order.
  • The overview map always shows the whole pattern, with the area marked, so you never lose your place.

Open questions

  • Should this replace the Spotlight focus block (which dims but does not clip), or sit alongside it?
  • Default margin: 0, 3 or 10 stitches?
  • Should the area be saved with the project and synced, as the focus block is now?
  • Do stitchers want free-form rectangles as well as block-aligned ones?

About the numbers

The draw readout times this prototype's renderer, to compare modes. stitchx already draws only the visible part of the chart, so its gain from a work area is mostly elsewhere: colour counts, highlight searches, bulk marks and pattern analysis all shrink from the whole pattern to the area. The readout's second line shows that scope.
