# Project materials and shopping lists

Open Stash Manager > Project materials. Save your charts first; unlinked library entries cannot make stock claims.

Owned is usable stock, including opened-skein allowances (mostly full 0.75, about half 0.5, remnant 0.25). Reserved is held for unfinished work. Available is owned minus all effective reservations, never negative. Estimated needed uses unfinished chart geometry, saved fabric count, over-two, working strands, blend proportions, tails and waste. It uses the durable-consumption model (1,890 single-strand inches per skein, approximately eight metres of six-strand cotton), not the older rounded Creator shopping estimate. Custom catalogue threads use the same standard skein capacity; convert non-standard pack lengths to standard skein equivalents. Beads and fabric are excluded.

Choose Reserve estimated needs, or set a non-negative fractional skein limit. Requests allocate in ascending project-ID order and are capped by needs, limits and live stock. This priority is deterministic across tabs. A request exceeding stock does not invent owned stock. Release reservations to free the claim. Changed charts and progress recalculate needs; completed, archived, abandoned (UFO) and deleted projects have no claims. Opening the materials view retires their reservation intent; after reopening a project choose Reserve again.

Every shopping calculation allocates remaining free stock once in the same order. Per-project lists show exact shortages; use the combined deduplicated list for purchases. Whole-skein rounding happens after adding shortages. With two projects needing 0.6 skeins each and 0.75 owned, the first reserves 0.6 and the second reserves 0.15: combined shortage 0.45, buy one skein. Adding one skein to stash clears the shortage. No purchase service or order ledger is involved.

Thread keys retain brands: dmc:310 and anchor:310 are different stock. Enter a catalogue identity and press Confirm substitution to replace a project's planning and subsequent consumption identity explicitly. The chart is unchanged. Remove the confirmed substitution before changing its replacement. Several original threads using the same replacement share its stock and one shopping row. Corrections return the actual recorded debit, even after the substitution is removed.

Reservations do not record consumption. Actual stitching uses the durable accounting ledger and cannot debit stock held by other projects. Imported historical progress remains a baseline. Refresh estimates after chart edits or purchases; exported lists are snapshots and do not record orders.

Export shopping list JSON includes quantities, units, project groups and combined purchases. Full .csb/JSON backups preserve reservation intent with stash and consumption. Backup export retires claims from local dashboard Archive/Complete states before reading the portable stash. Old stash records migrate through the existing brand-key migration without new stock debits or credits; legacy shopping flags remain independent. Project-only transfers do not carry stock claims. Sync treats reservation intent as part of the inventory snapshot and asks for reconciliation when plans conflict; use current clients on all devices.
